  (mentor_left, (31337_90806029597852, `Since the stack is so useful for programming, a "push" and "pop" instruction will be added to the next Symphony level. Both of these are synonyms for two other instructions and they both use the "sp" register (short for stack pointer) to keep track of the stack.

"push" is a synonym for:
sub sp, sp, 4
store_32 [sp], %value

"pop" is a synonym for:
load_32 %destination, [sp]
add sp, sp, 4

You may notice that "push" subtracts from "sp" so that the stack grows downwards from the high addresses. This leaves the low addresses for you to use as you see fit.`))
